# Code of conduct

DevLingo welcomes participation regardless of experience, identity or background. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, patient and constructive. Focus feedback on code and ideas, give credit, accept correction, and consider how your words affect others. Respect privacy and never disclose credentials or personal information without consent.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, doxxing and repeated disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

## Enforcement and reporting

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Responses should consider severity, repeated conduct and opportunities to correct behavior.

For an incident, request a private contact route from the maintainer without posting identifying or sensitive details publicly. A dedicated confidential conduct reporting channel has not yet been declared and should be configured before broader community rollout. Maintainers should protect reporter privacy and explain moderation decisions where doing so is safe.
